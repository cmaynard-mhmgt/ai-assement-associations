AI Readiness Self-Assessment Tool

How ready is your association for AI implementation?

This self-assessment is designed to help association executives and senior leadership teams develop an honest picture of where their organization stands across five dimensions of AI readiness. It is not a pass-or-fail instrument — it is a structured conversation starter designed to surface specific strengths and gaps that should inform your sequencing decisions as you move from AI awareness to AI implementation.

The assessment is most valuable when completed collaboratively by the senior leadership team rather than by a single individual. Different leaders will have different perspectives on the same questions, and those differences are themselves useful data about where organizational alignment work is needed before implementation begins.

1 – Not in place: Does not currently exist or apply to our organization.
2 – Early stage: We have begun thinking about this, but it is not yet functional.
3 – Developing: Partially in place but has meaningful gaps to be addressed.
4 – Well established: Functioning well and not a significant barrier to AI implementation.
